Five new models to switch between on the board: CRNNs on a gammatone spectrogram and their OR pairs with gbt_m1 - the candidates of the 2026-10-07/09 comparison, ported to C and held to their PyTorch modules.

The networks reach further and hold on to a moving drone better than the
layout-4 trees (out of fold at 1 false-alarm window per hour on val, means of
five seeds: a DJI at 60-90 m 77-89 % of windows against 73 % for gbt_m1's
recipe, the Runner 250 65-78 % against 24 %; a Runner flying in or out 72-89 %
against 11 %).

**Front end** (`src/gammatone.c`): per frame a periodic Hann window, its own
1024-point FFT, 64 gammatone filters on the ERB scale (`src/gammatone_table.h`,
generated by `bdtrain fixtures`) and a log, into a ring of the newest 31 rows
that every frame feeds, gated or not.

**Layouts** (`src/extractor_gs.c`): 703 = the 14 x 64 patch + layout 8 (layout 4
plus its ten modulation numbers over four seconds), 903 = the 31 x 64 patch
(one second) + layout 8, 603 = the 31 x 64 patch + layout 4. The envelope ring
holds four seconds now; layout 4 reads the newest two, bit for bit as before.
The hybrids do not wait for the ring: their networks were trained with the
modulation numbers missing, so they decide about 0.5 s (0.45 s patch) or 1.4 s
(1 s patch) into a run.

**Network** (`src/crnn.c`): conv 32 / conv 16 with batch norm and pooling, an
LSTM of 32, dense head; 47 k float32 weights, 1.26 M (0.45 s) to 2.88 M (1 s)
multiply-accumulates a window. Too heavy for one 21.33 ms mic block, so a model
can carry a job (`classifier_job_t`): with `boomdetect_config_t::job_budget`
the window closes `pending` and `boomdetect_work()` scores it in slices over the
next blocks. `detect` calls it every block (budget 200 k multiply-accumulates);
the DET line keeps the window's time, and with dbg a `DETDBG` line reports the
slowest PDM block, frame, slice and loop pass.

**OR pairs** (`src/classifier_pair.c`): gbt_m1 and a network on one window,
each score on its own false-alarm scale from the val negatives
(z = -log10((n + 1) / (N + 1))), the pair's decision the larger z - so a pair's
threshold is a z, 3.8 is about 1 false window an hour.

| model | what | default thr |
|---|---|---|
| `crnn_h` | CRNN, 0.45 s patch + layout 8 | 7.196 |
| `or_h` | gbt_m1 or crnn_h | z 3.831 |
| `crnn_hl` | CRNN, 1 s patch + layout 8 - the furthest reach | 8.774 |
| `or_hl` | gbt_m1 or crnn_hl | z 3.760 |
| `or_l` | gbt_m1 or a CRNN on the 1 s patch alone (crnn_l, not selectable) - no false alarm in the analysis | z 3.760 |

The thresholds are the 1 FA/h points on val (the other models ship at 5 FA/h).
The image grows to 1.0 MB of flash and 270 kB of RAM.
